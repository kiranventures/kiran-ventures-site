Privacy Policy

Kiran Ventures LLC · Last updated: July 2026

Overview

Dost (“the App”) is operated by Kiran Ventures LLC (“we”, “us”, “our”). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use Dost.

We built Dost to help you stay connected with the people who matter. We take your privacy seriously and collect only what is necessary to make the app work.

Information We Collect

Information you provide

  • Username and password — used to create and access your account
  • Phone number — used to verify your identity and connect you with friends who are already on Dost
  • Profile description — optional, visible to contacts who have added you

Information from your device

  • Contacts — we access your device contacts to identify which of your friends are on Dost and to present you with a list of contacts to add to your queue. Contact data is used solely for these purposes and is never sold or shared with third parties.
  • Photos — contact photos synced from your iOS contacts, and any profile photo you choose to upload

Information we collect automatically

  • Device information — device type, operating system version
  • Usage data — app sessions, feature usage, crash reports
  • Push notification tokens — used to send you reminders and notifications

Apple Sign In

If you choose to sign in with Apple, we receive a unique Apple user identifier and, on first sign in, your name and email address (or Apple relay address). We use this only to create and manage your account.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Match you with friends who are on Dost
  • Send you push notifications and reminders
  • Improve the app through crash reports and usage analytics
  • Respond to support requests

We do not:

  • Sell your data to anyone
  • Share your data with advertisers
  • Use your contacts for any purpose other than friend matching and contact selection

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase, which provides industry-standard encryption in transit and at rest. Contact data and personal information stored in Firestore is encrypted at the application level before being written to our database.

Third Party Services

Dost uses the following third party services:

  • Google Firebase — authentication, database, push notifications (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Apple Sign In — optional authentication (Apple Privacy Policy)

Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your personal data and contact associations are permanently deleted from our systems. Activity history shared with other users (such as connection logs) may be retained as part of those users’ accounts for the duration of their respective accounts.

You can delete your account at any time from Settings → Delete Account within the app.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at kiranventuresllc@gmail.com.

Children’s Privacy

Dost is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it immediately.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the app or by email. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
